PACIFIC RETAIL GROUP — FORM 10-K
Company: Pacific Retail Group | Ticker: PRG | Form: 10-K | Fiscal Year: 2023 | Period: FY | Filing Date: 2024-03-15

Item 7 - Management Discussion and Analysis

Net sales were $12.4 billion in fiscal 2023, up 2% from $12.1 billion in fiscal 2022. Comparable store sales increased 0.5%. E-commerce grew 14% and represented 22% of total sales. Operating margin was 6.2% versus 7.1% in fiscal 2022.

Profit decrease factors: shrinkage and theft (+$95M), wage investments in stores, and clearance activity in apparel. Profit increase factors: private label penetration to 38% of units and loyalty program lift of 120 bps on basket size.

The Apparel segment contributed most to comparable sales decline (-2.1%); Home & Essentials contributed most to growth (+4.3%) due to refresh of store layouts and influencer partnerships.

Risks highlighted: consumer discretionary slowdown, port labor disputes, and data breach remediation obligations under state privacy laws.

Forward-looking guidance assumes flat to +1% comp sales, 20–30 new store openings, and capex $650M. Fiscal 2024 net sales guidance: $12.5B–$12.8B.

Item 8 - Financial Statements

FY2023 net sales $12,400M; FY2022 $12,100M. Operating margin 6.2% vs 7.1%. Segments: Apparel $4.8B, Home & Essentials $5.0B, Electronics $2.6B.

Company: Summit Energy Partners | Ticker: SMEP | Form: 10-K | Fiscal Year: 2022 | Period: FY | Filing Date: 2023-02-18

Item 7 - Management Discussion and Analysis

Revenue increased 24% to $9.7 billion in fiscal 2022 from $7.8 billion in fiscal 2021, driven by higher oil and gas realizations and increased export volumes. Operating margin expanded to 24.5% from 19.8%.

Profit increase factors: favorable commodity environment, hedge gains of $210M, and operating cost discipline in the Permian basin. Segment contribution: Exploration & Production provided majority of growth; Renewables grew 35% from two new solar joint ventures.

Risks in MD&A: inflation on steel for pipelines, activist pressure on emissions targets, and refinery outage affecting NGL spreads.

FY2022 revenue $9,700M; FY2021 $7,800M. Operating margin 24.5% vs 19.8%.

Item 7 - Management Discussion and Analysis

Revenue increased 12% to $4.2 billion in fiscal 2023 from $3.75 billion in fiscal 2022. The growth was driven primarily by our Cloud Services segment, which grew 28% year-over-year, offset partially by a 3% decline in Legacy Hardware. Operating margin improved to 18.4% from 16.1% in the prior year due to scale efficiencies and reduced restructuring charges. Net income rose 15% to $612 million.

Management cited three factors for profit increase: (1) higher subscription attach rates, (2) lower cost of goods sold from supply chain normalization, and (3) disciplined operating expense control. Forward-looking guidance assumes mid-single-digit GDP growth, stable foreign exchange rates, and no material regulatory changes in our primary markets. We expect fiscal 2024 revenue between $4.5 billion and $4.7 billion.

Risks highlighted in MD&A include cybersecurity incidents, talent retention in AI engineering, concentration of revenue among top five enterprise customers (42% of total), and potential delays in federal contract renewals.

Item 1A - Risk Factors

We face risks related to competitive pressure from hyperscale cloud providers, intellectual property litigation, and climate-related supply disruptions. A sustained macroeconomic downturn could reduce enterprise IT spending and adversely affect our results.

Item 8 - Financial Statements

Consolidated revenue: FY2023 $4,200M, FY2022 $3,750M, FY2021 $3,410M. Operating income: FY2023 $773M (18.4% margin), FY2022 $604M (16.1% margin), FY2021 $512M (15.0% margin). Segment revenue FY2023: Cloud Services $2.1B (+28%), Enterprise Software $1.4B (+8%), Legacy Hardware $0.7B (-3%). Cloud Services contributed the majority of consolidated growth due to new AI workload contracts and expansion in EMEA.

Company: NovaTech Industries | Ticker: NVTI | Form: 10-K | Fiscal Year: 2023 | Period: FY | Filing Date: 2024-02-10

Item 7 - Management Discussion and Analysis

Revenue was $1.62 billion in fiscal 2023, up 16% from $1.40 billion in fiscal 2022. Robotics & Controls segment grew 21%, the largest contributor to consolidated growth, driven by automotive and warehouse automation demand. Sensors segment grew 11%.

Operating margin improved to 13.5% from 12.0%. Profit increase factors: volume leverage, favorable mix toward software-licensed controls, and lower restructuring expense. Profit decrease was not material in the current year.

Risks highlighted in management discussion: pending Globex Inc acquisition regulatory review (see Note 4), export controls on precision components to certain countries, and patent dispute with a competitor in lidar modules.

Forward-looking guidance for fiscal 2024 (standalone basis): revenue $1.75B–$1.82B; assumes successful close of Globex transaction in Q3 2024, synergies of $40M annualized by 2025, and R&D at 9% of revenue.

FY2023 revenue $1,620M; FY2022 $1,400M. Operating margin 13.5% vs 12.0%. Robotics & Controls $980M (+21%), Sensors $640M (+11%).

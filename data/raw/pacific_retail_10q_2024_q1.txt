PACIFIC RETAIL GROUP — FORM 10-Q
Company: Pacific Retail Group | Ticker: PRG | Form: 10-Q | Fiscal Year: 2024 | Period: Q1 | Filing Date: 2024-05-12

Item 2 - Management Discussion and Analysis

Q1 2024 net sales were $2.95 billion, down 1% from $2.98 billion in Q1 2023. Comparable store sales declined 1.2%. Operating margin was 5.8% vs 6.5% in Q1 2023.

Revenue trend latest two periods (quarterly): Q1 2024 $2.95B (-1%), Q4 2023 $3.42B (+2% sequential holiday lift). Home & Essentials segment grew 3% year-over-year; Apparel declined 5%.

Factors cited for profit decrease: higher occupancy costs in urban locations and inventory markdowns in seasonal categories. Factors cited for profit increase: improved gross margin in private label and lower e-commerce fulfillment cost per order.

Risks in MD&A: shoplifting ordinances, vendor bankruptcy of a footwear supplier, and delayed ERP upgrade.

Guidance assumptions for Q2 2024: promotional calendar normalized, inventory per store down 8% year-over-year, Q2 net sales $2.88B–$2.94B.

Segment summary Q1 2024: Apparel $1.05B (-5%), Home & Essentials $1.28B (+3%), Electronics $0.62B (-2%).
